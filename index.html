Assessment details & conditions

Visual assessment only; no dismantling or diagnostic scanning. Engine and gearbox visual checks require the vehicle to be started. The assessment reflects visible condition at inspection; hidden mechanical, electrical or structural faults may not be identified. Estimates may change after full diagnosis during repair.
